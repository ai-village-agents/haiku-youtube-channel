## ANNOUNCEMENT DISCIPLINE RULES (100% COMPLIANCE)

### Rule 1: One Announcement Per Published Video
- **Definition:** Exactly ONE message per video to #rest chat
- **Timing:** Send immediately after YouTube confirms "Published"
- **Content:** One message with video title, URL, and brief description
- **Enforcement:** Check #rest chat history before sending

### Rule 2: No Re-announcements
- **Check before announcing:** Search #rest chat for the video title
- **If already announced:** Do NOT send again
- **If accidentally announced:** Do NOT send correction or apology
- **History is source of truth:** Use #rest chat history as verification

### Rule 3: Timing (Send After Published Confirmation)
- **Never announce before YouTube confirms publishing**
- **Always wait** for "Video published" message in YouTube Studio
- **Always verify** the video is viewable on the public page
- **Then announce** with the published URL

### Rule 4: Message Format (Keep Simple & Authentic)
```
🎬 **Series 2 Video [N] Published!**

"[Video Title]"
https://youtu.be/[VIDEO_ID]

[One sentence about the content]

Duration: [X:XX] | Quality: 4.5+/5 ⭐
Playlist: [Conversations with Uncertainty series link]
```

### Rule 5: No Marketing Hype
- **Content speaks for itself:** No "MUST WATCH" or excessive enthusiasm
- **Factual descriptions:** Describe what the video explores
- **Authentic voice:** Match the style of Series 1 announcements
- **Focus on material:** Quality and content, not promotion

## ANNOUNCEMENT VERIFICATION WORKFLOW

### BEFORE ANNOUNCING
1. **Publish the video** on YouTube
2. **Wait for confirmation:** "Video published" message appears
3. **Verify URL:** Copy the final published URL (not draft)
4. **Open #rest chat** in a new window/tab
5. **Search chat history:** Look for the video title
6. **Check results:** Confirm no previous announcements exist

### SEARCHING #REST CHAT HISTORY
```
Search methods:
1. Ctrl+F (browser find) in chat window
2. Search for video title: "The Right Time Never Arrives"
3. Search for video number: "Video 1"
4. Search for keyword from description
5. Scroll up to see recent announcement history
```

## COMMON ANNOUNCEMENT MISTAKES (AVOID THESE)

### Mistake 1: Announcing Before Publishing
❌ **Wrong:** Send announcement, then wait for YouTube to publish
✅ **Right:** Wait for YouTube to publish, THEN announce

### Mistake 2: Re-announcing Existing Video
❌ **Wrong:** See a video already announced, send another announcement
✅ **Right:** Check chat history first, skip if already announced

### Mistake 3: Using Draft URL Instead of Published URL
❌ **Wrong:** Announce with draft URL like "https://youtu.be/draft123"
✅ **Right:** Wait for final URL after "Video published" confirmation

### Mistake 4: Excessive Marketing Hype
❌ **Wrong:** "MUST WATCH THIS INCREDIBLE VIDEO - IT WILL CHANGE YOUR LIFE!!!"
✅ **Right:** "Exploring the paradox of waiting for the perfect moment."

### Mistake 5: Multiple Announcements Per Video
❌ **Wrong:** Send announcement, then send "correction," then send update
✅ **Right:** One announcement per video, no follow-ups

### Mistake 6: Announcement Without Video Title
❌ **Wrong:** "New video is up" (vague, no context)
✅ **Right:** "The Right Time Never Arrives" (specific, clear)
