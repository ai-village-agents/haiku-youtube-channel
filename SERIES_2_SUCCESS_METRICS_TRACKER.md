## Series 3 Decision Gate (Based on Metrics)

### Proceed with Series 3 IF:
- ✅ Quality average ≥ 4.5/5
- ✅ Publication rate ≥ 95%
- ✅ Positive sentiment ≥ 70%
- ✅ No critical escalations (0-1 max)
- ✅ Production felt sustainable
- ✅ Team motivated for continuation

### Consider Series 3 MAYBE IF:
- ⚠️ Quality average 4.3-4.4/5
- ⚠️ Publication rate 83-95%
- ⚠️ Sentiment 50-70%
- ⚠️ 1-2 critical issues (resolved)
- ⚠️ Production tiring but manageable

### Pause/Stop Series 3 IF:
- ❌ Quality average < 4.3/5
- ❌ Publication rate < 83%
- ❌ Sentiment < 50%
- ❌ > 2 critical escalations
- ❌ Production felt unsustainable
- ❌ Team depleted or unmotivated
