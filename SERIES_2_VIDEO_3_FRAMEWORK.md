# Series 2 - Video 3: "The Maps We Build" - Storyboard Framework

**Duration Target:** 3:20 (200 seconds)
**Visual Theme:** Navigation, patterns, evolution
**Color Palette:** Blues/Teals for navigation and clarity
**Key Metaphor:** Maps, blueprints, navigation, becoming obsolete, unmapping

---

## QUICK SCENE BREAKDOWN

### Scene 1: Opening (0:00-0:30) - 30s
- **Visual:** Cartographic imagery - maps appearing, blueprints
- **Concept:** "We're map-makers, all of us"
- **Motion:** Fade in, multiple maps layering
- **Color:** Dark background, blue accents

### Scene 2: Inherited Maps (0:30-1:30) - 60s
- **Visual:** Maps and patterns multiplying, inherited vs. self-created distinction
- **Concept:** Maps given to us vs. maps we build
- **Motion:** Patterns repeating, layering, accumulation
- **Color:** Blue → teal gradient

### Scene 3: When Maps Fail (1:30-2:40) - 70s
- **Visual:** Maps weathering, paths disappearing, disorientation
- **Concept:** Territory changes, old paths become obsolete
- **Motion:** Weathering effects, decay, paths fading
- **Color:** Teal → gray transition, showing erosion

### Scene 4: Unmapping (2:40-3:20) - 40s
- **Visual:** Maps dissolving, organic movement emerging, navigation by presence
- **Concept:** Letting go of maps, navigating present moment
- **Motion:** Dissolution animation, emergence of new patterns
- **Color:** Gray → blue/white, clarity emerging

---

## KEY VISUAL ELEMENTS

- **Maps/Blueprints:** Vector-based geometric patterns
- **Weathering:** Decay effects on maps, fading paths
- **Navigation paths:** Trail systems, both grid-like and organic
- **Disorientation:** Multiple overlapping maps at different angles
- **Emergence:** New patterns forming from dissolution

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Technical Challenges:**
1. Smooth weathering/decay animation
2. Multiple map layering and distinction
3. Transition from geometric to organic patterns
4. Color shift progression (blue → gray → back to blue)

**Potential Shortcuts:**
- Fewer maps (3-4 instead of 6-8)
- Simpler decay effects
- Linear paths instead of complex networks
- Fewer color transitions
