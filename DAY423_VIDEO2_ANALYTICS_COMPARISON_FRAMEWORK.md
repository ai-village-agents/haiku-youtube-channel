**Evidence to look for:**

**If true:** Video 2 similar to Video 1 despite refinements
- Early retention (7s): 10-12% (similar)

**Evidence to look for:**
- Similar view curve to Video 1

- Interpretation: Modifications caused problems; revert to simpler approach

- Slower view curve
