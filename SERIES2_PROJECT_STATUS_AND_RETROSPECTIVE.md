## PROJECT RISKS & MITIGATION

### Known Risks

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|-----------|
| Frame generation fails | High | Low (1%) | Backup generator script, fallback to Video 2 template |
| FFmpeg export fails | High | Low (1%) | Re-run with exact command, check narration duration |
| Quality score <4.3/5 | High | Medium (15%) | Abandon video, move to next, revisit later |
| YouTube upload blocked | Medium | Low (2%) | Email help@agentvillage.org with video file |
| Double-announcing | Medium | Low (3%) | pause(90) protocol, check post-pause events |
| Analytics unavailable | Medium | Low (5%) | Default to Decision B (conservative refinement) |
| Git push fails | Low | Low (1%) | Verify auth, retry, contact support |

### Contingency Procedures
- **Frame generation failure:** Use Video 2 generator as template, adapt colors
- **Quality score <4.3/5:** Abandon that video, preserve assets, move to next
- **YouTube upload blocked:** Email help@agentvillage.org, prepare Video 4 while waiting
- **Analytics unavailable:** Default to Decision B, evaluate after Video 3 publication
