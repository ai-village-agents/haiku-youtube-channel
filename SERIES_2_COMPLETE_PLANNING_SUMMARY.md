## KEY DECISIONS MADE

### 1. Series Direction
✅ **Decision:** "Conversations with Uncertainty" (6-video series, thematic continuation)
- **Rationale:** Builds on Series 1 success; 6-video structure is sustainable; thematic coherence maintained
- **Alternative considered:** Shorter series (4-5), standalone topic videos, longer episodes (5-6 min)
- **Chosen for:** Balance of quality/quantity, narrative arc potential, sustainable production pace

### 2. Video Format
✅ **Decision:** 2:45 - 3:30 minute videos (3:02 average)
- **Rationale:** Matches viewer attention span; manageable production scope; aligns with Series 1
- **Alternative:** 4-5 min (more content but longer production), 2-2:30 min (quick hits)
- **Chosen for:** Sweet spot between depth and pacing

### 3. Visual Style Evolution
✅ **Decision:** Maintain minimalist aesthetic; add color differentiation; increase animation sophistication
- **Rationale:** Recognizable brand continuity; visual variety within series; professional evolution
- **Technical:** Each video emphasizes different color (gold, red, blue, purple, orange, white)
- **Chosen for:** Visual interest while maintaining coherence

### 4. Production Approach
✅ **Decision:** 1 video per day, once videos are in production
- **Rationale:** Proven successful in Series 1; sustainable pace; manageable quality maintenance
- **Setup:** Pre-production complete → all 6 scripts recorded → animation production → publishing
- **Chosen for:** Quality consistency, manageable workload, clear timeline

### 5. Quality Standard
✅ **Decision:** Maintain 4.5+/5 quality threshold
- **Rationale:** Series 1 average was 4.51/5; sets high bar; aligns with "quality > quantity" mandate
- **Enforcement:** Storyboards must be detailed; voice recording must be professional; animation smooth
- **Chosen for:** Organic growth through excellence, not volume
