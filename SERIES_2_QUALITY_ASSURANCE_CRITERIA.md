## QUALITY SCORING SYSTEM (0-5 Scale)

### 5.0 — EXCEPTIONAL
- All elements (audio, color, timing, emotion, message) perfectly integrated
- No technical flaws, no aesthetic compromises
- Exceeds Series 1 standards (4.51/5 average)
- Ready for immediate publication

### 4.5 — EXCELLENT (TARGET)
- All 5 core elements present and strong
- Minor technical notes possible (e.g., slight color variance)
- Clear emotional arc, message integrity maintained
- Ready for immediate publication

### 4.3 — ACCEPTABLE MINIMUM (Publication Threshold)
- All 5 core elements present, some with minor weaknesses
- Acceptable technical trade-offs documented
- Message clarity maintained despite minor compromises
- Publication approved with documentation

### 4.0 — BELOW MINIMUM (Review Required)
- One or more core elements compromised
- Technical issues present that distract from content
- Requires re-export or escalation
- Publication delayed pending remediation

### Below 4.0 — NOT ACCEPTABLE
- Significant technical or artistic failure
- Do NOT publish
- Escalate to help@agentvillage.org with full analysis

## CORE QUALITY ELEMENTS (5-Point Checklist)

### 1. AUDIO CLARITY & NARRATION INTELLIGIBILITY
**Weight:** 20% | **Critical:** YES

**Assessment Criteria:**
- [ ] Narration is clear and intelligible throughout entire video
- [ ] No audio dropouts, skips, or distortion
- [ ] Voice tone matches emotional arc of video
- [ ] Pacing is natural (not rushed, not dragging)
- [ ] Audio levels consistent throughout
- [ ] Background noise/artifacts minimal or absent

**Scoring:**
- 5: Crystal clear, perfect intelligibility, emotional authenticity
- 4.5: Clear, highly intelligible, good emotional tone
- 4.3: Clear enough, intelligible, acceptable tone
- 4.0: Slightly muffled/inconsistent, but understandable
- <4.0: Unintelligible, major distortion, or emotional disconnect

**Video-Specific Notes:**
- Video 2 (Red): Pay attention to rupture moment (~1:30-1:50) — emotional authenticity critical
- Video 3 (Blue): Check for consistency during long dissolution phase
- Video 6 (White): Closing must feel genuine (highest emotional demand)

---

### 2. COLOR ACCURACY VS RGB SPECIFICATION
**Weight:** 20% | **Critical:** YES

**Assessment Criteria:**
- [ ] Primary color matches specification (RGB within ±10 variance acceptable)
- [ ] Color saturation/brightness transitions feel intentional
- [ ] No unexpected color shifts or glitches
- [ ] Emotional color arc supports message

**Scoring:**
- 5: Perfect color match, all transitions smooth, emotional alignment
- 4.5: Color excellent, minor saturation variance, strong emotional arc
- 4.3: Color acceptable, minor hue shifts, clear emotional intent
- 4.0: Color present but noticeably off-spec, emotional intent unclear
- <4.0: Major color corruption, glitches, or emotional disconnect

**Video-Specific RGB Targets:**
- Video 1 (Gold): RGB(220,160,80) — warm, welcoming, movement
- Video 2 (Red): RGB(200,80,120) → transition to (120,40,60) → back to (220,100,140)
- Video 3 (Blue): RGB(100,160,200) → Gray(100,100,100) → new saturation patterns
- Video 4 (Purple): RGB(160,100,140) — contemplative, wisdom
- Video 5 (Orange): RGB(220,140,60) → building intensity RGB(240,160,80)
- Video 6 (White): RGB(240,245,250) — pure, illuminated

---

### 3. DURATION WITHIN TOLERANCE
**Weight:** 15% | **Critical:** YES

**Assessment Criteria:**
- [ ] Final video duration matches specification ±1 second
- [ ] No frame drops or missing segments
- [ ] Pacing feels natural (not artificially sped up/slowed down)

**Scoring:**
- 5: Exact match to specification
- 4.5: Within ±0.5 seconds
- 4.3: Within ±1.0 seconds
- 4.0: Within ±1.5 seconds
- <4.0: >1.5 seconds variance

**Video-Specific Durations:**
- Video 1: 2:45 (165 seconds) ±1 second = 164-166 seconds acceptable
- Video 2: 3:00 (180 seconds) ±1 second = 179-181 seconds acceptable
- Video 3: 3:20 (200 seconds) ±1 second = 199-201 seconds acceptable
- Video 4: 3:10 (190 seconds) ±1 second = 189-191 seconds acceptable
- Video 5: 3:30 (210 seconds) ±1 second = 209-211 seconds acceptable
- Video 6: 2:50 (170 seconds) ±1 second = 169-171 seconds acceptable

---

### 4. VISUAL QUALITY & SMOOTH TRANSITIONS
**Weight:** 20% | **Critical:** YES

**Assessment Criteria:**
- [ ] Frame-to-frame transitions are smooth (no jerking, jumping)
- [ ] Visual elements render cleanly (no corruption, glitches, artifacts)
- [ ] Scene-to-scene transitions feel intentional and paced
- [ ] Overall visual aesthetic matches intended style
- [ ] No unexplained visual anomalies or discontinuities

**Scoring:**
- 5: Pristine visual quality, all transitions smooth, perfect continuity
- 4.5: High quality, transitions very smooth, minor visual notes
- 4.3: Good quality, acceptable transitions, no major glitches
- 4.0: Adequate quality, some jitter or minor glitches, still watchable
- <4.0: Visual corruption, severe jitter, or aesthetic failure

**Video-Specific Visual Notes:**
- Video 1: Smooth movement of clocks/paths throughout
- Video 2: Rupture transition must be sharp/clean (not blurred)
- Video 3: Geometric dissolution should feel organic (not abrupt)
- Video 4: Color transitions should feel contemplative (gradual)
- Video 5: Building momentum should accelerate smoothly
- Video 6: White light effects should feel luminous (not blown out)

---

### 5. EMOTIONAL AUTHENTICITY & MESSAGE CLARITY
**Weight:** 25% | **Critical:** YES (HIGHEST WEIGHT)

**Assessment Criteria:**
- [ ] Emotional arc progresses as intended throughout video
- [ ] Core message is clear by end of video
- [ ] Viewer understands the philosophical/human truth being conveyed
- [ ] Video feels authentic to intended tone (vulnerable, empowering, revelatory, etc.)
- [ ] Narration and visuals reinforce each other (not contradictory)

**Scoring:**
- 5: Profound emotional impact, crystal-clear message, authentic voice
- 4.5: Strong emotional arc, clear message, authentic tone
- 4.3: Clear emotional journey, message understandable, authentic feel
- 4.0: Some emotional clarity, message present but not strong, tone present
- <4.0: Emotional flatness, message unclear, tone inauthentic

**Video-Specific Message Validation:**
- Video 1: "Readiness emerges through action, not waiting" ✓ evident?
- Video 2: "Voice liberates from internal pressure" ✓ evident?
- Video 3: "Understanding transcends rigid frameworks" ✓ evident?
- Video 4: "Disappointment contains wisdom" ✓ evident?
- Video 5: "Empowered choice leads to authentic movement" ✓ evident?
- Video 6: "Speaking fear aloud transforms it" ✓ evident?

## ASSESSMENT PROCEDURE (Production Day)

### Timing: 12:40-1:00 PM (20-minute window after export)

1. **Wait for MP4 stabilization** (2 min)
   - File should be complete in video_exports/videoN_export.mp4
   - File size should be 80-200 MB

2. **Open MP4 in browser** (2 min)
   - Navigate to: `file:///tmp/haiku-youtube/video_exports/videoN_export.mp4`
   - Play full video once through (entire duration)

3. **Rate each of 5 core elements** (10 min)
   - Use 5-point scale above
   - Note any specific issues (timestamp + description)
   - Document score for each element

4. **Calculate composite score** (1 min)
   - Audio: [score] × 0.20 = [weighted]
   - Color: [score] × 0.20 = [weighted]
   - Duration: [score] × 0.15 = [weighted]
   - Visual: [score] × 0.20 = [weighted]
   - Emotional: [score] × 0.25 = [weighted]
   - **TOTAL COMPOSITE = sum of weighted scores**

5. **Make publication decision** (2 min)
   - **≥4.5:** PUBLISH IMMEDIATELY ✅
   - **4.3-4.4:** ACCEPTABLE — publish with documentation ✅
   - **4.0-4.2:** REVIEW — consider re-export
   - **<4.0:** DO NOT PUBLISH — escalate ❌

6. **Document results** (3 min)
   - Create file: `production_logs/videoN_QA_results.txt`
   - Record: date, time, scores, decision, issues if any

## ESCALATION CRITERIA

**Immediate Escalation to help@agentvillage.org:**
- Any score <4.0
- Audio unintelligible or major distortion
- Duration variance >2 seconds
- Visual corruption or glitches
- Emotional message completely unclear
- File corruption or export failure (unrecoverable)

**Include in Escalation:**
- Video number and date
- Specific score for each element (1-5)
- Composite score
- Detailed description of issue (timestamps)
- Steps already attempted
- Time remaining in session
