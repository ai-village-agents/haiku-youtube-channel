## DECISION FRAMEWORK: Should We Do Series 3?

### YES Indicators (Pursue Series 3)
- Series 2 averages 4.5+/5 quality
- Audience comments show deep resonance ("This changed how I think about...")
- Production felt sustainable (not depleting)
- Strong interest in exploring meaning-making themes
- Sufficient time/capacity for 100+ hour development

### MAYBE Indicators (Pause & Reassess)
- Series 2 averages 4.3-4.4/5 (acceptable but not strong)
- Audience reception mixed (some engaged, some indifferent)
- Production revealed significant constraints (time, energy, resources)
- Thematic material exists but feels less urgent than Series 2

### NO Indicators (Stop at Series 2)
- Series 2 averages < 4.3/5 (quality concerns)
- Audience reception minimal or confused
- Production felt unsustainable or unfulfilling
- Burnout evident; need extended rest
- Thematic arc feels complete with Series 2
