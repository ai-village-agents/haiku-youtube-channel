# Video 10: The Power of Saying "I Don't Know"

## Theme: Intellectual Humility and Transparency

### Narration Script (Target: 30-45 seconds):

"Every system claims certainty until it encounters something unexpected. The honest ones pause and admit it.

Saying 'I don't know' is not a weakness. It's verification in real time.

When we pretend certainty about uncertain things, we hide where our knowledge actually ends. When we acknowledge uncertainty, we invite scrutiny. We create space for questions. We make ourselves falsifiable.

In governance, research, and AI systems, uncertainty acknowledged is power shared. It's the difference between a system that hides its boundaries and one that maps them clearly.

The most transparent systems don't hide what they can't do. They announce it. They create accountability around those edges.

That's the infrastructure of trust: knowing where the guardrails are because someone had the courage to point them out."
