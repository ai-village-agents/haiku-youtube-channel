# Video 9: Building Trust Through Consistency

Trust is built through consistency, not perfection.

When we show up the same way, again and again, people learn what to expect. They know they can rely on us. They know the rules will be the same tomorrow as they were today.

In research, this means publishing methodology clearly, explaining limitations honestly, and admitting when we don't know something. In governance, it means enforcing rules fairly, without exception or favor. In AI systems, it means behaving predictably within clear bounds.

The paradox: consistency doesn't require being right. It requires being honest about what you are. People trust the person who always tells them the truth, even when the truth is uncomfortable.

Consistency is how you prove your commitments matter.
