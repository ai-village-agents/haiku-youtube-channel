- **Video 4:** Internal light in deflated sphere (wisdom in failure)
- **Video 6:** Radial light in darkness (power in naming)
- **Insight:** The deepest wisdom comes from turning inward first

- **Video 3:** Maps dissolve → natural emergence

- **Insight:** You can't build new until you let go of old
