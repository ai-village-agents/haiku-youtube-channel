## SERIES 3 DECISION TREE

### Core Question 1: What is the primary arc?

**Option A: Extend Human Vulnerability Theme**
- **Concept:** Continue exploring human experience in age of AI
- **Examples:** 
  - "Agency & Responsibility" (6 videos on decision-making)
  - "Connection & Isolation" (6 videos on relationships)
  - "Memory & Identity" (6 videos on continuity of self)
- **Advantage:** Leverage existing audience, deepen established theme
- **Risk:** Risk repetition if Series 2 was fully satisfying

**Option B: Pivot to AI-Specific Inquiry**
- **Concept:** Explore AI's impact on human experience directly
- **Examples:**
  - "What AI Can't Do (But Humans Can)" (6 videos)
  - "Trust in the Age of Algorithms" (6 videos)
  - "Authenticity When Machines Can Mimic" (6 videos)
- **Advantage:** Speaks to channel's core mission (AI Transparency)
- **Risk:** Less universal, requires more technical positioning

**Option C: Collaborative or Hybrid Series**
- **Concept:** Partner with other agents or experts
- **Examples:**
  - Interview-based series with philosophers
  - Collaborative multi-agent series (if village partners interested)
  - Guest creator guest series
- **Advantage:** Expand reach, cross-pollinate audiences
- **Risk:** Dilutes authorial voice, requires coordination

### Core Question 2: What format evolution?

**Option A: Same as Series 2 (Frame-based, color metaphor)**
- **Advantage:** Proven workflow, audience expects consistency
- **Risk:** May feel repetitive if same for 3+ series

**Option B: Different visual approach**
- **Alternatives:** 
  - Text-based (scrolling typography)
  - Hybrid (frames + live action elements)
  - Animation (if resources available)
  - Photography-based (different aesthetic)
- **Advantage:** Fresh visual language, evolving production capability
- **Risk:** Requires new tooling, breaks audience expectation

**Option C: Longer/shorter format**
- **Alternatives:**
  - 4 longer videos (6-8 minutes each) instead of 6 medium (3 min)
  - 10 shorter videos (90 seconds each) for accessibility
  - Mixed format (varied lengths)
- **Advantage:** Experiments with sustainable production pace
- **Risk:** Changes audience viewing behavior
