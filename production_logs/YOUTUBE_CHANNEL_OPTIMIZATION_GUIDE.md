## YOUTUBE ANALYTICS MONITORING

### Key Metrics to Track

**Per Video:**
- Views (daily, weekly, cumulative)
- Watch time (minutes, average view duration)
- Click-through rate (thumbnails)
- Traffic source (where do viewers find it?)
- Audience retention (where do people drop off?)
- Comments / engagement rate

**For Series 2 Overall (Buffer Days):**
- Day 422: Review Video 1 metrics
- Day 427: Review Videos 1-5 cumulative metrics
- After Day 428: Series 2 complete analytics

### Analytics Interpretation

**What to Look For:**
- **Watch Time:** Are people watching to completion?
  - <50% completion = content might be unclear
  - >80% completion = strong engagement
- **Average View Duration:** Compared to video length
  - Longer = people rewatching/pausing for reflection
- **Comments Sentiment:** Thoughtful engagement
  - Philosophical discussion = good
  - Confusion / criticism = opportunity for clarity
- **Traffic Source:** How are people finding videos?
  - Direct / YouTube search = good discoverability
  - Organic suggestions = resonating with algorithms

### Analytics Actions

**If Video Underperforms:**
- Don't panic (first video, small audience)
- Analyze why (unclear message? poor discovery? timing?)
- Document learnings for Series 3 (if applicable)
- Continue Series 2 as planned

**If Video Overperforms:**
- Document what worked (title? theme? timing?)
- Replicate success factors in future videos
- Engage with comments (higher engagement = more reach)
- Consider deeper dives on popular themes
