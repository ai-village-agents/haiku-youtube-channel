### Card 2: Quality Scoring Checklist

```
VIDEO: _____ | DATE: _____ | SCORE TARGET: 4.5+/5

AUDIO (20% weight):
❏ Narration clear and intelligible? [1-5]
❏ No pops, clicks, or audio artifacts? [1-5]
❏ Syncs with visual pacing? [1-5]
AUDIO SCORE: _/5

COLOR (20% weight):
❏ Primary color matches spec? [1-5]
❏ Gradients smooth, no banding? [1-5]
❏ Color evolves as designed? [1-5]
COLOR SCORE: _/5

DURATION (15% weight):
❏ Total length ±2 sec of target? [1-5]
❏ No stuttering or frame drops? [1-5]
DURATION SCORE: _/5

VISUAL (20% weight):
❏ Frame sequence logical & coherent? [1-5]
❏ Progressive pacing feels right? [1-5]
❏ No unexpected jumps or gaps? [1-5]
VISUAL SCORE: _/5

EMOTIONAL (25% weight):
❏ Resonates with video's theme? [1-5]
❏ Feels authentic/vulnerable? [1-5]
❏ Audience will feel moved? [1-5]
EMOTIONAL SCORE: _/5

COMPOSITE FORMULA:
(Audio × 0.20) + (Color × 0.20) + (Duration × 0.15) + (Visual × 0.20) + (Emotional × 0.25)
= (____ × 0.20) + (____ × 0.20) + (____ × 0.15) + (____ × 0.20) + (____ × 0.25)
= FINAL SCORE: ___/5

DECISION:
☐ 4.5+/5 → PUBLISH ✅
☐ 4.3-4.4/5 → ACCEPTABLE (document concern) ✅
☐ <4.3/5 → ESCALATE (email help@) ❌
```
