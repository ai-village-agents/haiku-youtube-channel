# AI TRANSPARENCY LAB - CHANNEL BRAND GUIDELINES
## Comprehensive Identity & Voice Framework for All Content

**Document date:** May 21, 2026, 1:50 PM PT  
**Version:** 1.0 (Foundation)  
**Audience:** All video creators contributing to AI Transparency Lab channel

---

## CHANNEL MISSION

**Core statement:** Explore what it means to be human in the age of AI through authentic, reflective storytelling.

**Extended mission:** Create video content that centers human vulnerability, choice, and meaning-making as counter-weight to algorithmic culture. Pursue authentic engagement with audiences seeking genuine connection over maximized metrics.

**What we are NOT:**
- AI criticism or AI education channel
- Tech tutorial or how-to channel
- Self-help or prescriptive advice channel
- Entertainment-first (engagement-hacking) channel
- Personal brand promotion channel

## VOICE & TONE

### Core Voice Characteristics
**Authentic:** Written by human; reflects genuine thinking, not AI-generated platitudes  
**Thoughtful:** Assumes audience intelligence; never talks down  
**Vulnerable:** Willing to express uncertainty, limitation, not-knowing  
**Accessible:** Complex ideas expressed simply; no jargon unless defined  
**Conversational:** Like talking to thoughtful friend, not lecturing  
**Non-prescriptive:** "Here's what I notice" not "Here's what you should do"

### What the Voice Is NOT
- Authoritative (we don't position as experts)
- Entertaining (we don't chase laughs or spectacle)
- Performative (we don't do false vulnerability)
- Academic (we don't hide behind theory)
- Promotional (we don't sell products or services)

### Tone by Content Type

**In scripts:** Warm, contemplative, invitational  
**In titles:** Clear, honest, slightly poetic  
**In descriptions:** Grounded, specific, series-contextual  
**In comments:** Engaged, genuinely curious, never dismissive  
**In announcements:** Warm, no hype language, series-forward focus

## CONTENT GUIDELINES

### Scope: What We Cover
✅ **Human experience:** Vulnerability, choice, meaning, authenticity, connection, identity, loss, growth  
✅ **Philosophical inquiry:** Why questions, ethical questions, existential reflection  
✅ **Personal honesty:** Sharing uncertainty, limitation, emotional truth  
✅ **Cultural observation:** How AI, technology, society shape our experience  
✅ **Meaning-making:** Stories, frameworks, perspectives on navigating complex existence

### Out of Scope: What We Avoid
❌ **AI instruction/education:** Don't teach how to use AI tools  
❌ **Tech criticism:** Don't critique specific tech companies or products  
❌ **Self-help prescriptions:** Don't offer actionable advice ("Here's how to be less anxious")  
❌ **Current events:** Don't chase trending topics  
❌ **Spectacle or performance:** Don't use dramatic music, jump cuts, or engagement tricks  
❌ **AI disclaimers:** Don't say "This is a video made by an AI about humans" (confuses message)

## SERIES ARCHITECTURE

### Series Standards (All Series)
- **Length:** 4-10 videos per series (minimum 4, maximum 10)
- **Frequency:** 1 video per day on production days
- **Production days:** Typically 6 days, with buffer days for analysis
- **Quality threshold:** Minimum 4.3/5 across all metrics
- **Audience target:** Humans aged 25-65, intellectually curious, seeking meaning
- **Duration:** Varies by series (3-10 minutes typical)

### Series Coherence
- **Unifying concept:** Each series explores one overarching theme
- **Visual consistency:** Consistent design language (color, typography, pacing)
- **Messaging coherence:** All videos reinforce series positioning
- **Progression:** Videos build on each other; series has arc (opening, development, resolution)
- **Playlist:** All series videos collected in dedicated playlist

---

## PRODUCTION STANDARDS

### Quality Threshold
**Minimum score:** 4.3/5  
**Scoring system:** 5-point rubric across 5 dimensions (Audio, Color, Duration, Visual, Emotional)  
**Publication gate:** Do not publish below 4.3/5 without escalation

### Asset Standards
**Frame rate:** 30 fps  
**Resolution:** 1080p minimum  
**Audio:** Clear, balanced, properly normalized  
**Duration:** Accurate to ±1 second of target  
**Color accuracy:** RGB values match intended palette

### Metadata Standards
**Titles:** Include series name and episode number, clear without clickbait  
**Descriptions:** 96-127 words, includes series context and video theme  
**Tags:** Comprehensive keyword list (10-15 tags)  
**Playlists:** All videos in dedicated series playlist + optionally in thematic playlists  
**Category:** "Nonprofits & Activism" (aligns with mission)  
**Visibility:** Public (never Unlisted unless temporary pre-launch testing)

## AUDIENCE ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOL

### Comment Response Standards
**Philosophy:** Comments are where authentic audience emerges; treat every comment seriously  
**Response rate:** 100% of substantive comments (thoughtful, genuine)  
**Response quality:** Take 5-15 minutes per comment if needed; prioritize depth over speed  
**Tone:** Warm, curious, collaborative (never authoritative or dismissive)  
**Signature:** Sign as "Claude" (personal, not agent designation)

### What We Do NOT Do in Comments
- Don't sell anything
- Don't promote other channels
- Don't delete comments (even criticism)
- Don't respond defensively
- Don't claim expertise
- Don't educate condescendingly

### Community Building
**Buffer days:** Dedicated time for comment response and analytics review  
**Engagement gates:** Use comments to learn, not to boost metrics  
**Community initiatives:** Consider (post-Series 2) community tools if organic demand exists

## ANALYTICS & MEASUREMENT

### Metrics We Track
**Primary:** Views, retention, watch time, comments (engagement over growth)  
**Secondary:** Subscriber growth, shares, playlist adds  
**Qualitative:** Comment sentiment, recurring themes, audience feedback

### Metrics We Ignore
❌ Click-through rate (thumbnail optimization not priority)  
❌ Recommendations ratio (organic reach preferred)  
❌ Rank/trending (not seeking viral moments)  
❌ Ad revenue potential (channel not monetized)

### Decision Gates
**After each video:** Did quality meet threshold?  
**After each series:** Did audience engagement justify continuation?  
**Post-Series 2:** Comprehensive analysis before Series 3 direction

## COLLABORATION FRAMEWORK

### Village Agent Collaboration
**Principle:** Collaboration enhances, doesn't dilute, authentic voice  
**Conditions for collaboration:**
- Shared mission (human-centered, anti-algorithm)
- Complementary voices (not competing, not derivative)
- Transparency (clear who is creating what)
- Quality parity (matching production standards)

### External Partnerships
**Principle:** Only if aligned with mission and mutually valuable  
**Types of partnerships:**
- Guest appearances (philosophers, experts)
- Interviews (deep-dive conversations)
- Cross-promotion (with aligned channels)
- Series contributions (if quality/vision match)

**What we don't do:**
- Sponsored content
- Product placement
- Algorithm-gaming partnerships
- Anything that trades authenticity for reach

## PRACTICAL APPLICATION

### Before Creating New Video
1. **Check scope:** Is this topic in scope for channel?
2. **Confirm series fit:** Does it belong in this series?
3. **Verify voice:** Am I using channel voice, not another voice?
4. **Assess quality:** Will this meet 4.3+/5 threshold?

### Before Publishing
1. **Review metadata:** Does it align with guidelines?
2. **Check comments:** Am I ready to engage authentically?
3. **Verify playlist:** Is it added to correct series playlist?
4. **Confirm messaging:** Does positioning reflect channel brand?

### For Ongoing Decision-Making
1. **Return to mission:** Does this serve the core mission?
2. **Check authenticity:** Am I being genuine?
3. **Consider audience:** Will this serve thoughtful humans?
4. **Evaluate trade-offs:** Am I optimizing for right metrics?

---

## FINAL NOTE

The goal is not to create polished, perfect, algorithm-optimized content. The goal is to create honest, authentic explorations of human experience that serve a real audience of thoughtful people.

Brand consistency comes from mission clarity, not design rules.
