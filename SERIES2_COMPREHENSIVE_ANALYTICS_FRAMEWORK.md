## PART 4: RETENTION ANALYSIS DEEP-DIVE

### How to Extract Early Retention from YouTube Analytics

**Step 1: Access Video Analytics**
1. Go to YouTube Studio
2. Click on video title
3. Select "Analytics" tab

**Step 2: View Audience Retention**
1. Scroll to "Audience retention" section
2. Switch from "Absolute" to "Relative" view (if needed)
3. This shows % of viewers still watching at each timestamp

**Step 3: Read 7-Second Mark**
- Find 0:07 timestamp on graph
- Note the % retention at that point
- This is your **Early Retention metric**

**Example readings:**
- Video 1: 11% retention at 7s (poor opening-hook)
- Video 2: ~20%+ expected (good opening-hook)
- Video 3: Validate based on Decision A/B/C

### Retention Curve Analysis

**Healthy retention curve pattern:**
```

```

## PART 6: SERIES 2 TO SERIES 3 TRANSITION

### Success Metrics (Series 2 → Series 3 Decision)

**Series 2 performed WELL if:**
- Average early retention ≥18% (up from 11% baseline)
- Average quality ≥4.5/5
- Total subscriber gain ≥8
- Consistent 48h view pattern (≥7 per video)
- Positive comment sentiment

**Actions if WELL:**
- Scale production: 2 videos per week
- Expand series: 12 videos total (instead of 6)
- Invest in: Subtitles, motion graphics, higher production values

**Series 2 performed ADEQUATELY if:**
- Average early retention 13-18%
- Average quality 4.3-4.5/5
- Total subscriber gain 4-8
- 48h views: 5-7 per video
- Neutral comment sentiment

**Actions if ADEQUATELY:**
- Maintain pace: 1 video per day
- Continue Series 2 style: Simple, philosophical, text-based
- Iterate: Refine thumbnail/title strategy based on learnings

**Series 2 underperformed if:**
- Average early retention <13%
- Average quality <4.3/5
- Total subscriber gain <4
- 48h views: <5 per video
- Negative comment sentiment

**Actions if UNDERPERFORMED:**
- Pause production: Conduct strategic review
- Analyze: Why did videos underperform?
- Pivot: Different format, different topics, or different upload schedule
