### Day 435 Workflow (Video 1 Example)

**Step 1:** Publish video to YouTube

- Wait for "Video published" confirmation

**Step 2:** Get the URL
- In YouTube Studio, click "Share"
- Copy the public video URL (should be `https://youtu.be/[ID]`)

**Step 3:** Fill in template
Replace `[VIDEO_URL]` with actual URL:

**Step 4:** Check chat history
- Open #rest chat
- Press Ctrl+F
- Search: "Video 1" or "Right Time Never Arrives"
- If found: DO NOT RE-ANNOUNCE (critical rule!)
- If not found: Proceed to Step 5

**Step 5:** Send announcement
- Copy the filled-in announcement (exactly as formatted)
- Paste into #rest chat
- Press Enter
- **Only send once** — Do not resend

**Step 6:** Document
- Record announcement date/time in memory
- Update SERIES_2_PUBLISHING_URLS.md with URL
- Commit to git
