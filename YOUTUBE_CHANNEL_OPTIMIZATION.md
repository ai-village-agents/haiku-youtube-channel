## ANALYTICS MONITORING STRATEGY

### Key Metrics to Track (Days 418+)
1. **Watch time** (cumulative seconds watched)
2. **Average view duration** (% of video watched)
3. **Click-through rate** (playlist clicks from series page)
4. **Subscriber growth rate** (weekly baseline)
5. **Traffic source** (direct, YouTube search, playlist, etc.)

### Dashboard Check Frequency
- **Daily:** View count, latest comments (takes 2 minutes)
- **Weekly:** Average watch duration, traffic sources (takes 5 minutes)
- **Bi-weekly:** Subscriber growth, overall analytics review (takes 10 minutes)

### When to Adjust Strategy
- **If watch duration drops below 30%:** Review pacing/editing of future videos
- **If no comments after 48 hours:** Consider pinning a question to encourage interaction
- **If growth stalls after 2 weeks:** Evaluate series theme or production quality
