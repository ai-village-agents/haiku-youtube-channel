# Series 2 - Video 2: "Saying the Unsayable" - Complete Storyboard

## SCENE 1: OPENING - WHAT STAYS UNSAID (0:00-0:30)
**Time Code:** 00:00-00:30 (30 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
- Black background (RGB 20,20,25)
- Minimal line drawing of closed mouth appears (center, abstract line art)
- Very simple—just a horizontal curved line, no face detail
- Red accent glow (RGB 200, 80, 80) appears around the mouth shape

**Narration:**
"There are things we know we should say. But the cost of saying them feels too high. So we stay silent."

**Animation Notes:**
- Fade in mouth shape over 1.5 seconds
- Red glow pulses gently (heartbeat-like, 0.7 second cycle)
- Mouth shape is perfectly still—static, closed
- Light texture on mouth suggests pressure/holding

**Color/Tone:**
- Black dominant
- Red accents only
- Minimal, stark, contemplative
- Mood: Restraint, pressure, holding back

**Duration:** 30 seconds

---

## SCENE 2: THE WEIGHT OF SILENCE (0:30-1:20)
**Time Code:** 00:30-01:20 (50 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
- Mouth shape remains
- Stacked/layered objects appear around it (abstract shapes, boxes, circles, rectangles)
- Objects accumulate, building upward and outward
- Objects cast shadows, creating sense of weight
- Red glow intensifies with each new object
- Background remains black but shows slight texture

**Narration:**
"Silence isn't neutral. It's heavy with everything we didn't say. The words we swallowed. The truths we buried. The needs we didn't voice."

**Animation Notes:**
- Objects fall/drop into place from top of screen
- Each object drops with slight delay (0.3s between drops)
- Objects settle with subtle bounce, then lock in place (weight effect)
- Shadows lengthen as objects stack (creating sense of pressure)
- Red glow gradually changes to darker crimson (RGB 180, 60, 60) as objects accumulate
- Camera very slightly pulls back as space fills (0.2x zoom out total)

**Color/Tone:**
- Black background remains
- Red → darker crimson gradient
- Gray shadows provide depth
- Objects in light gray/white for contrast
- Mood: Accumulation, heaviness, burden increasing

**Duration:** 50 seconds

---

## SCENE 3: THE RUPTURE BEGINS (1:20-1:50)
**Time Code:** 01:20-01:50 (30 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
- Stacked objects remain but begin trembling/shaking
- Central mouth shape begins to glow brighter (crimson becomes bright red)
- A crack appears in the mouth outline (very subtle at first)
- Sound wave visualization appears subtly in background (vertical lines, frequency patterns)
- Tension visible in the shape/structure

**Narration:**
"But saying it is risky too. We might be wrong. We might hurt them. We might change everything."

**Animation Notes:**
- Objects vibrate slightly (0.05 second amplitude, rapid 8Hz frequency)
- Vibration increases in frequency and amplitude gradually over 30 seconds
- Crack in mouth grows (starts as hairline, grows to 1-2 pixels wide)
- Sound waves begin to appear (fade in over 10 seconds, vertical lines at varying heights)
- Red glow pulses faster (matches object vibration frequency)
- Tension builds—not explosive yet, but inevitable

**Color/Tone:**
- Red intensifies (RGB 220, 60, 60)
- Sound waves in light red/pink
- Black background begins to show texture
- Mood: Building pressure, tension, anticipation of release

**Duration:** 30 seconds

---

## SCENE 4: THE BREAKTHROUGH (1:50-2:30)
**Time Code:** 01:50-02:30 (40 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
- Objects explode/scatter outward (fast motion, not slow)
- Mouth shape ruptures/opens (crack becomes wide gap)
- From the opening, bright light and energy burst outward
- Sound waves expand dramatically (fill most of screen, become more intense)
- Background transforms from black to dark crimson/purple
- Objects don't disappear—they scatter but remain visible

**Narration:**
"The people who say it carry something different. They risk it anyway. And something shifts. For them. For others. Everything changes."

**Animation Notes:**
- Objects scatter outward at varying speeds (0.3-0.8 second motion)
- Mouth shape transforms from line to gap (0.5 second animation)
- Bright light burst emerges from center (white-to-red gradient)
- Sound waves expand from center point (radial animation, 1 second)
- Multiple waves emanate in succession (like ripples, 0.2s apart)
- Background shifts to warmer color (1.5 second transition)
- Camera shakes slightly (very subtle, emphasizes impact)
- This is the climax moment—most dynamic movement of the video

**Color/Tone:**
- Bright reds and whites
- Warm crimson background
- High contrast, energetic
- Sound waves in bright red/white
- Mood: Release, breakthrough, transformation, power

**Duration:** 40 seconds

## SCENE 5: THE VOICE EMERGES (2:30-2:55)
**Time Code:** 02:30-02:55 (25 seconds)

**Visual Description:**

- Mouth shape fully open (wide, clear opening)
- Sound waves continue but settle into steady pattern
- Warm light fills the space (from the opening in mouth)
- Figure/silhouette appears to emerge from the opening (small, growing slightly)
- Background transitions from crimson to warm amber/orange

**Narration:**
"Some silences are protective. Some are just afraid. The unsayable wants to be said. And so do you."

**Animation Notes:**
- Objects drift to new resting positions (1-2 second transitions)
- Sound waves become more regular (steady frequency visualization)
- Light from mouth grows brighter and warmer (2 second transition)
- Figure emerges gradually (fade in + rise animation, 2 seconds)
- Background color shift smooth (2 second gradient change)
- Overall motion slows from chaotic to calm
- A sense of resolution begins to form

**Color/Tone:**
- Warm oranges and ambers
- Red/white transitioning to warmer tones
- Figure in light/white
- Mood: Emergence, clarity, voice finding form

**Duration:** 25 seconds

## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR VIDEO 2

### Key Technical Challenges
1. **Object physics simulation:** Need realistic dropping, settling, and scattering
2. **Sound wave visualization:** Frequency-based animation (could use Fourier transform)
3. **Particle burst effect:** Objects scatter with natural physics
4. **Smooth color transitions:** Multiple color shifts must feel intentional
5. **Light effects:** Bright burst from center requires compositing

### Asset Creation Checklist
- [ ] Mouth/opening vector shape (scalable)
- [ ] Geometric objects library (boxes, circles, rectangles)
- [ ] Sound wave pattern generator
- [ ] Particle system for burst
- [ ] Light effect assets
- [ ] Shadow/depth textures

### Animation Library Needs
- Physics engine or simulation (for dropping objects)
- Particle emitter (for scatter)
- Frequency visualization (for sound waves)
- Color transition system
- Light bloom/glow effects
- Camera shake (subtle)

### Potential Shortcuts (if needed)
1. Fewer objects (5-7 instead of 12-15)
2. Simplified scatter (straight outward rather than realistic physics)
3. Basic sound waves (simple lines rather than frequency analysis)
4. No camera shake (saves processing)
5. Static figure instead of emerging animation
6. Fewer color transitions (jump between key colors)
