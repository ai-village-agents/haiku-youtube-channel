## KEY QUALITY THRESHOLDS (CRITICAL UNDERSTANDING)

### 4.5/5 - TARGET QUALITY (MUST PUBLISH AT THIS LEVEL OR HIGHER)

**What makes a 4.5/5 video:**
- Technical quality: Good encoding, no major errors, proper specs met
- Visual quality: Colors accurate, transitions smooth, meets all specifications
- Narrative quality: Clear voice, appropriate pacing, emotional authenticity present
- **The 4.5 Test:** "This video meets Series 1 standards and conveys the message effectively"

**Technical Components (4.5 assumes):**
- ✓ H.264 High Profile, 1920×1080, 30fps, AAC 192kbps
- ✓ Duration within 1 second of target (e.g., Video 1 at 2:45±1s)
- ✓ File size 50-75 MB
- ✓ No encoding errors or artifacts
- ✓ Audio clear, no clipping, properly synced

**Visual Components (4.5 assumes):**
- ✓ Colors match RGB specification (e.g., Gold 220,160,80)
- ✓ Smooth transitions between scenes
- ✓ No flickering, banding, or temporal artifacts
- ✓ Scene composition matches storyboard intent

**Narrative Components (4.5 assumes):**
- ✓ Narration is clear and intelligible
- ✓ Voice tone is consistent and appropriate
- ✓ Pacing is natural, not rushed or dragging
- ✓ Message is conveyed effectively
- ✓ Emotional authenticity is present (viewer can feel it's genuine)

**Series 1 Comparison:** 4.5/5 = Match Series 1's proven 4.51/5 average

---

### 4.3/5 - MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE QUALITY (EMERGENCY FALLBACK ONLY)

**What makes a 4.3/5 video:**
- Technical quality: Acceptable encoding, may have minor issues
- Visual quality: Colors acceptable, transitions acceptable, meets minimum specs
- Narrative quality: Voice clear, pacing acceptable, message conveyed
- **The 4.3 Test:** "This video works and conveys the message, but is not ideal"

**Technical Components (4.3 allows):**
- ✓ H.264/AAC encoding, proper codec (but minor quality loss acceptable)
- ✓ Duration within 2 seconds of target (e.g., Video 1 at 2:43-2:47)
- ✓ File size 45-80 MB (slightly wider range)
- ✓ Minor encoding artifacts acceptable (but not visible to average viewer)
- ✓ Audio clear enough (but may not be studio-perfect)

**Visual Components (4.3 allows):**
- ≈ Colors approximately match RGB spec (within 10 points acceptable)
- ≈ Transitions mostly smooth (minor roughness acceptable)
- ≈ No major artifacts (very minor banding acceptable in background)
- ≈ Scene composition mostly matches storyboard

**Narrative Components (4.3 allows):**
- ≈ Narration is understandable (but may have minor clarity issues)
- ≈ Voice tone is mostly consistent
- ≈ Pacing is acceptable (but may feel slightly rushed or slow)
- ≈ Message is conveyed (but less elegantly than 4.5)
- ≈ Emotional authenticity is present but may feel slightly muted

**4.3 Use Cases:**
- Frame generator produces acceptable result but not perfect
- Color specs have minor variance
- Narration has slight technical issues but is still understandable
- Only use if re-export would not improve (time constraint)

---

### 4.0/5 - MARGINAL QUALITY (CONSIDER RE-EXPORT)

**What makes a 4.0/5 video:**
- Technical quality: Some quality loss, encoding has issues
- Visual quality: Colors may be off, transitions rough
- Narrative quality: Voice acceptable but not ideal, pacing could be better
- **The 4.0 Test:** "This video has visible problems that should be fixed"

**When to Re-Export at 4.0/5:**
- Color shift noticeable to viewer
- Transitions have visible jitter or artifacts
- Narration has audio issues (slight clipping, background noise)
- Duration is off by 3+ seconds
- Message is clear but delivery feels awkward

---

### BELOW 4.0/5 - UNACCEPTABLE (DO NOT PUBLISH)

**Never publish below 4.0/5. Instead:**
1. Identify the problem (frame generator failure? color spec issue? audio problem?)
2. Attempt fix or re-export
3. If fix not possible within remaining time, escalate to help@agentvillage.org

## QUALITY GOTCHAS TO AVOID

1. **Don't trust browser playback** — Use ffprobe/ffmpeg to verify specs
2. **Don't assume colors are right** — Sample RGB values from exported file
3. **Don't publish if narration feels off** — Authenticity matters
4. **Don't accept artifacts** — Frame quality should match Series 1
5. **Don't rush quality assessment** — Take 10-15 minutes per video
