## CATEGORY 1: HOOK (30% weight)

**Question:** Does the first 7 seconds compel viewers to keep watching?

### Evaluation Criteria (0-5 scale)

**5/5 - EXCELLENT:**
- Immediate visual attention-grabber
- Narration opening is clear and compelling
- Audio/visual elements work in perfect harmony
- Viewer is drawn in immediately; "must keep watching" feeling
- Example: Strong color, provocative opening line, clear purpose

**4/5 - GOOD:**
- Strong opening with good visual presence
- Narration is engaging and clear
- Audio/visual elements work together well
- Viewer interested but not overwhelming pull
- Example: Solid graphics + clear voice, good setup

**3/5 - ADEQUATE:**
- Adequate visual setup, some visual interest
- Narration is functional but not particularly compelling
- Audio/visual elements present but not tightly integrated
- Viewer may or may not continue based on other factors
- Example: Basic visuals + adequate narration

**2/5 - WEAK:**
- Slow or confusing visual start
- Narration opening unclear or poorly delivered
- Audio/visual elements feel disconnected
- Viewer might lose interest before hook lands
- Example: Confusing graphics + unclear narration

**1/5 - POOR:**
- Actively confusing or off-putting opening
- Narration unclear or difficult to hear
- Audio/visual elements clash or confuse
- Viewer likely to click away
- Example: Bad audio quality, incoherent visuals

**0/5 - FAILURE:**
- Completely fails to engage
- Narration inaudible or nonsensical
- Visual elements are broken or missing
- Technical failure or completely disengaging

### Specific Questions to Answer:
1. Does the first frame grab attention? (Color, composition, clarity)
2. Is the opening narration clear and compelling?
3. Do audio levels work from the very start?
4. Is there a clear sense of direction/purpose in first 7 seconds?
5. Would YOU keep watching past 7 seconds?

## CATEGORY 3: PRODUCTION (20% weight)

**Question:** Is the audio/visual execution professional and polished?

### Evaluation Criteria (0-5 scale)

**5/5 - EXCELLENT:**
- Audio is crystal clear; perfectly balanced levels
- No audio artifacts (pops, clicks, distortion)
- Transitions are seamless (0.5s cross-fades smooth)
- Video has no glitches, artifacts, or quality drops
- Timing/sync is perfect (±0ms)
- Professional studio-quality execution throughout

**4/5 - GOOD:**
- Audio is clear with good balance
- Minimal or no audio artifacts
- Transitions are smooth and well-timed
- Video quality is consistent
- Timing/sync is tight (±50ms)
- Professional quality with minor nits

**3/5 - ADEQUATE:**
- Audio is acceptable, levels generally okay
- Minor audio artifacts present
- Transitions are present but could be smoother
- Video quality is generally consistent
- Timing/sync is acceptable (±100ms)
- Adequate professional quality

**2/5 - WEAK:**
- Audio issues are noticeable (levels off, some artifacts)
- Transitions are abrupt or poorly timed
- Video has noticeable glitches or quality drops
- Timing/sync problems noticeable
- Production quality feels amateurish
- Distracting issues present

**1/5 - POOR:**
- Significant audio problems (distortion, clipping, imbalance)
- Transitions are choppy or missing
- Video has significant artifacts
- Major timing/sync issues
- Production feels very amateur
- Multiple distracting issues

**0/5 - FAILURE:**
- Audio is unusable
- Video is broken or unwatchable
- No transitions or complete technical failure
- Complete sync failure
- Essentially unwatchable

### Specific Questions to Answer:
1. Can you hear narration clearly at consistent levels?
2. Is the background music at appropriate level (not overwhelming)?
3. Are transitions smooth or do they feel abrupt?
4. Is video quality consistent throughout? (No drops or artifacts)
5. Does narration sync with on-screen visuals? (±100ms acceptable)
