# PHASE 5 QUALITY SCORING - VIEWING PREPARATION

**Time:** After Phase 4 (visual refinement complete)  
**File:** video2_export_FINAL.mp4 (or video2_export_POLISHED.mp4 if Opus uses same file)

---

## PRE-VIEWING CHECKLIST

- [ ] Close all other applications to minimize distractions
- [ ] Prepare scoring rubric (VIDEO2_QUALITY_SCORING_PHASE5.md) on second monitor/window
- [ ] Test audio output (speaker/headphone volume adequate)
- [ ] Plan first viewing: Watch start-to-finish without pausing
- [ ] Plan second viewing: Note timestamps for problem areas (if any)
- [ ] Have scoring template ready to fill in

---

## FIRST VIEWING - OVERALL IMPRESSION (3 minutes)

**Goal:** Get uninterrupted sense of the video's pacing, hook strength, emotional arc

**Watch for:**
- Does the first 7 seconds grab your attention?
- Does the narrative flow make sense without rewinding?
- Are transitions smooth and well-timed?
- Is audio clear and balanced throughout?
- What's your overall emotional impression?

**Note:** Don't score yet, just observe

---

## SECOND VIEWING - DETAILED EVALUATION (5-7 minutes)

**Pause at key moments:**

### Hook Evaluation (0:00-0:07)
- Pause at 0:07
- Score Hook category (0-5)
  - First frame compelling?
  - Narration opening effective?
  - Audio/visual synergy?

### Content Evaluation (Full length)
- Watch entire video, note logical flow
- Are key ideas reinforced visually?
- Is message clear and coherent?
- Emotional resonance present?
- Score Content category (0-5)

### Production Evaluation (Full length)
- Note any audio issues (pops, distortion, level problems)
- Check for visual glitches or artifacts
- Are transitions smooth (0.5s cross-fades)?
- Is timing sync ±100ms?
- Score Production category (0-5)

### Value Evaluation (Full length)
- Is this perspective original/unique?
- Will viewers learn something new?
- Is there transformative insight?
- Rewatchable?
- Score Value category (0-5)

---

## SCORING PROTOCOL

**Use rubric:** VIDEO2_QUALITY_SCORING_PHASE5.md

**Both evaluators:**
1. Watch video twice independently
2. Fill in scores for each category (0-5)
3. Provide written feedback on each category
4. Calculate final score: (Hook × 0.30) + (Content × 0.35) + (Production × 0.20) + (Value × 0.15)

**Decision threshold:** ≥4.3/5 to publish

---

## COMMON SCORING MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Don't be too harsh on minor imperfections (use 4/5 for small nits)
- Don't score based on concept alone (judge actual execution)
- Don't let one weak category drag down entire score if others are strong
- Be consistent across categories (compare rubric benchmarks)
- Document specific examples in feedback (timestamps, observations)

---

## SCORING TIPS

- **4.5+/5 videos** have: professional execution + clear message + good hook + unique value
- **4.0-4.4/5 videos** have: solid execution + good message but minor issues
- **3.5-3.9/5 videos** have: adequate execution but notable issues or unclear messaging
- **Below 3.5/5** videos: need significant rework before publishing

---

## POST-SCORING DECISION WORKFLOW

### IF ≥4.3/5:
1. Celebrate! 🎉
2. Prepare YouTube upload (YOUTUBE_UPLOAD_CHECKLIST_VIDEO2.md)
3. Begin Phase 6A (upload process)

### IF <4.3/5:
1. Identify specific refinement areas (use feedback from both evaluators)
2. Create REFINEMENT_PLAN.md with concrete improvement steps
3. Schedule second polish session (recommend same day if time permits)
4. Do NOT proceed to YouTube upload
