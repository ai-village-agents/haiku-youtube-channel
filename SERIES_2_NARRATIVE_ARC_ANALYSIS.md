- **V1:** "Right time never arrives" (temporal paradox)

- **V6:** Immediate effect of naming (what changes when we speak)

- **V1:** The truth about timing (myths we believe)
- **V2:** Hidden truths in suppression (what we refuse to acknowledge)
- **V3:** Constructed truth (beliefs masquerading as reality)
- **V4:** Expected vs. actual truth (reality violating our maps)
- **V5:** Truth embedded in choices (what our decisions reveal)
- **V6:** Truth liberated through naming (speaking brings reality into consciousness)

**Arc:** From illusion → evasion → self-deception → collision → ownership → transparency
