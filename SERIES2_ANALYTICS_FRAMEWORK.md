### Retention Curve Interpretation
**Healthy Pattern:**
- Steep initial drop (expected for casual viewers)
- Plateau after opening hook period
- Gradual decline toward end
- 10%+ completion rate indicates strong engagement

**Unhealthy Pattern:**
- Steep drop continuing past 7s
- No plateau (continuous decay)
- <5% completion rate
- Suggests content quality issue, not discovery issue

## ENGAGEMENT METRICS BEYOND RETENTION

### Watch Time
- **Metric:** Total hours watched
- **Calculation:** Views × Avg Duration
- **Interpretation:** Combined volume + engagement indicator
- **Video 1 Baseline:** 7 views × ~1.5 min avg = ~10.5 minutes watch time
- **Video 2 Target:** ≥15 views × ≥2 min avg = ≥30 minutes watch time

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- **Metric:** Clicks on video thumbnail ÷ Impressions
- **Baseline:** Varies by search term, recommendation quality
- **Interpretation:** Title/thumbnail effectiveness
- **Improvement Path:** If early retention improves, CTR may increase (more views from recommendations)

### Subscriber Conversion
- **Metric:** New subscribers ÷ Total views
- **Video 1 Rate:** 2 subs ÷ 18 views = 11.1% (completers only)
- **Target:** Maintain 8-12% conversion rate
- **Interpretation:** Quality signal - high conversion = strong viewer satisfaction

### Search Performance
- **Metric:** Views from search, top search queries
- **Interpretation:** SEO effectiveness for philosophical video topics
- **Example searches:** "philosophy," "existential," "silence," "authenticity"
- **Opportunity:** Title/description optimization based on search queries
